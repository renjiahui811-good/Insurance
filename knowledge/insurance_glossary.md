# Singapore Insurance Glossary

A quick reference for common Singapore insurance terms and abbreviations.

## Health & Hospitalisation

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| MediShield Life | Basic national health insurance run by CPF Board, covers large hospital bills (Class B2/C wards). |
| Integrated Shield Plan (IP / ISP) | Private insurance that sits on top of MediShield Life for higher ward classes / private hospitals. |
| Rider (IP) | Add-on that covers the deductible and co-insurance of an Integrated Shield Plan. |
| Deductible | Amount you pay first each policy year before the IP pays out. |
| Co-Insurance | Percentage of the bill you share with the insurer after the deductible. |
| Co-Payment | Fixed portion of the claim the policyholder pays, common in newer riders. |
| Panel Doctor | Approved provider offering pre-agreed fees; using non-panel may raise co-payment. |
| Pro-Ration | Bill scaling when treated in a ward class higher than the policy covers. |

## Life & Protection

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Sum Assured | Guaranteed payout amount on a claim (death, TPD, CI). |
| Term Life | Pure protection for a fixed period, no cash value. |
| Whole Life | Lifelong coverage with a cash value component. |
| Critical Illness (CI) | Lump-sum payout on diagnosis of a covered illness (e.g. cancer, heart attack). |
| Early / Intermediate CI | Payout at earlier stages of a critical illness. |
| Total & Permanent Disability (TPD) | Payout if permanently unable to work / perform daily activities. |
| Disability Income (DI) | Monthly income replacement if unable to work due to disability. |
| Personal Accident (PA) | Coverage for injury, disability, or death from accidents. |

## Savings & Investment

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Endowment | Savings plan that pays a lump sum at maturity, often with guaranteed + bonus. |
| Investment-Linked Policy (ILP) | Combines insurance with investment in sub-funds; value varies with markets. |
| Guaranteed Value | Portion of cash value the insurer contractually guarantees. |
| Non-Guaranteed / Bonus | Additional value dependent on the insurer's performance. |
| Participating (Par) Fund | Pooled fund whose surplus is shared with par policyholders as bonuses. |

## Premiums & Admin

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Premium | Amount paid to keep the policy in force. |
| Premium Term | How long premiums are paid (e.g. limited pay, single pay, whole of life). |
| CPF MediSave | CPF account usable to pay MediShield Life and IP base premiums (up to limits). |
| Free-Look Period | Window (usually 14 days) to cancel a new policy for a refund. |
| Waiting Period | Time after policy start before certain claims are payable. |
| Nomination of Beneficiary | Naming who receives the payout (Revocable / Irrevocable / Trust). |
| PDPA | Personal Data Protection Act — governs handling of client personal data. |
| Free-Look / Surrender Value | Amount received if a policy is cancelled or surrendered early. |
